    The rulebook as coded

    RULE 1

    Take the trade that feels difficult

    A good entry usually feels uncomfortable: buying after several hard down days, or selling after a sharp rally. Measured with 2-day RSI. Buy side: RSI(2) ≤ 15. Sell side: RSI(2) ≥ 85.

    RULE 2

    Fade the short-term trend when it fights the long-term trend

    Long-term trend is the 200-day average, short-term is the 10-day. Buy side: close > 200d and close < 10d (pullback in an uptrend). Sell side: close < 200d and close > 10d (bounce in a downtrend).

    RULE 3

    Know where the crowd's stops are

    Most traders put stops just past the most recent swing. Buy side: within the last 3 sessions price traded below the prior 10-day low, so those stops are flushed. Sell side: price traded above the prior 10-day high. Our own stop sits 0.25 ATR beyond the swept extreme.

    Rules come from the caption of the coingrams reel quoting David Paul (MoneyShow). The thresholds above are this tracker's own reading of them, not his published parameters.
